LEVEL 01

Two clearly-separate chambers joined by a single corridor. Each room is a bounded space with pillars and wall blockers that break the straight line — the player has to weave up and over to cross. Only two sprites are fixed: guide-bot on the route through the Antechamber, and the EMBERLING boss battle in the Sanctum. Lore, props and lights get placed later.

FIXED SPRITES

THE TWO CHAMBERS

ANTECHAMBER — warm bounded room. Two wall blockers sit on the spine and a central pinch funnels the player, so crossing means weaving up-and-over rather than walking straight. guide-bot stands on the route just past the pinch.

SANCTUM — colder boss hall. A colonnade of pillars plus an approach blocker bend the walk toward the EMBERLING battle at the far end. A black void + lone corridor keep the two rooms unmistakably separate.
