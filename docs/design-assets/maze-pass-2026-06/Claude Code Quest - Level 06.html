> THE THRONE

LEVEL 06★ FINALE

The trophy room. Not a maze — a stage. One long ceremonial aisle runs east between two colonnades of pillars and a gauntlet of braziers to the dais, where THE OVERLORD — the dragon — waits on its throne above a crimson summoning circle. No NPCs, no lore. Every other room teaches; this one judges. Fall here and you respawn at the door.

THE STAGE

WHY IT’S THE FINALE

One chamber, no branches. The room funnels you down a single sacred corridor — colonnades narrow the path, braziers light the march, and the dragon grows in your view with every step. The walk itself is the ceremony.

A battlefield’s afterimage. Cracked bricks, rubble and the bones of failed heroes litter the floor; chains hang from the rafters; crimson runes pulse behind the throne. Ozymandias meets a server room.

The end gate. Beyond the throne, a gold-framed END door stays sealed until the Overlord falls and drops the final key — the only locked door in the game that opens onto the credits.
